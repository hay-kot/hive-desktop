The third onboarding step asks to **Allow notifications**. Clicking it triggers
the operating system's permission prompt — approve it there.

## Why it matters on macOS

Until you grant the OS permission, Hive **cannot raise system banners at all**.
Activity still lands in the Activity view and as in-app toasts while Hive is
focused, but nothing surfaces in the background. The app never re-prompts
mid-use, so if you skip the prompt during onboarding, grant it later under
**Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ System permission** — the **Allow notifications**
button appears there while the permission is still *Not requested*.

You can **Skip for now** and keep using the app; you'll just miss background
banners until you enable them.

## Delivery modes

Once permission is granted, tune when banners appear under
**Settings ▸ Notifications**:

- **Automatic** — a system banner only when Hive is unfocused (the default).
- **Always a system banner** — a banner even when Hive is in front.
- **Always in Hive** — never a banner; in-app toast only.

There's also a master **Enable notifications** toggle and a **Play sound**
option.
