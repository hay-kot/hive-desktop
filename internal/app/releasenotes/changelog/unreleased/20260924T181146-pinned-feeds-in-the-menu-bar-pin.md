**Pinned feeds in the menu bar.** Pin up to three feeds in Settings ▸ Menu bar to list their newest items in the menu bar dropdown. Each item opens in the browser or in Hive, copies its link, or runs any configured action that needs no further input. A Profiles submenu opens a profile in Hive. The menu no longer turns profiles on and off; do that from the profile's settings.
